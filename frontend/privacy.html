Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 1, 2026

This is a plain-language explanation of what data When To Look collects, why we collect it, and what happens to it. No legalese.

What we collect

  • Email address — required to send you notifications. That's the whole point.
  • Location (latitude, longitude, and city name) — used only to compute which sky events are visible from where you are. We don't track your movements; you give us one location when you sign up.
  • Notification preferences — which event types you've opted into (ISS passes, meteor showers, aurora alerts, etc.).
  • Notification history — a log of which emails we've sent you. This is used purely to avoid sending you the same alert twice.

What we do not collect

  • Passwords (there's no account — no login, no dashboard)
  • Payment information (the service is free)
  • Browsing history, page analytics, or tracking data of any kind
  • Anything beyond what's listed above

We use no cookies, no tracking pixels, and no third-party analytics scripts. If you view the source of this page, you won't find any.

How we use your data

We use it for one thing: sending you the sky event notifications you signed up for.

We never sell, share, rent, or trade your data with third parties. Your email and location will never appear in a marketing list or be used for any purpose other than the alerts you asked for.

We use AWS SES to deliver email. This means your email address passes through Amazon's servers in transit. Amazon's privacy policy governs their handling of that data.

How to delete your data

Click the Unsubscribe link at the bottom of any email we send you. This permanently removes your email address, location, and all associated preferences from our systems. There's no waiting period — it happens immediately.

Alternatively, email us at hello@whentolook.com and we'll manually delete your data within 48 hours.

Cookies

None. We don't use cookies, session storage for tracking, or any persistent identifiers. The site works without any of that.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change what data we collect or how we use it, we'll email you before the change takes effect. You'll have the option to unsubscribe at that point if you're not comfortable with the change.

Questions?

Email us: hello@whentolook.com
